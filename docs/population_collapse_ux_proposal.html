The cold a band dies of

Two reporting options, side by side, driven by the shipped arithmetic.

issue #431 · docs/plan_population_collapse.md · research prototype, nothing here is implemented

A band dies at 6.5 °C — the morale break-even, where the climate drain overtakes the settling gain. Output floors at ×0.5, an equipped gatherer drops from 0.400 food/worker to 0.200 against a break-even of 0.2298, and the band declines for ever. Every lethality surface in the HUD keys off a different number: 0.0 °C, where cold starts killing people directly.

These two thresholds were half a degree apart until recently

cold.onset_temp was 6.0 °C — so the tile warning covered the morale cliff almost exactly. It was lowered to 0.0 °C in 84777e06 (“6 °C was never cold enough to kill anyone”), a reasonable playtest call about deaths. Nothing moved the morale lever with it, and the gap opened behind the change. The stale comment in hud_selection_vocab.gd still says “people start dying at 6.0 °C”.

The alternative to option 1 is a model change: move cold.onset_temp back up to meet the morale cliff, or move the morale lever down to meet the death onset, so there is one line again. Surface B · the death feed

The rung ladder is already right: bracket=working and bracket=child claim RUNG_ALERT, bracket=elder stays Notable on purpose. What it never receives is a worker. push_demographic_events pools deaths onto one carry and labels the event with the largest-contributing bracket, and the flat elder_mortality_rate of 0.20 wins that comparison until about −17.3 °C.

Nothing in hud_event_vocab.gd changes in any of these. Only which event the sim emits.

3c promote on cause None — client-side rung rule One row in DETAIL_STATUS_STYLE Yes Breaks the ladder's own rule that a death's rung is its bracket, and an elder dying of cold would alert — the case #614 deliberately left on Notable

3a is the recommendation. It is the smallest change, it needs no client edit, it preserves the pooled carry's head-count exactness, and it does not touch the bracket rule the ladder is built on.

Reading the two surfaces together

They fail independently and they compound. At 3 °C the card says temperate, the feed says old age, and the band is on a curve to zero. Fixing either one makes the situation legible; fixing surface A makes it legible before anybody has died, which is the only point at which the player can still walk the band somewhere else.
