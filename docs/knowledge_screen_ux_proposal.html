The glyph, and the icon that should replace it

⌃ was lame. The bar's existing marks are deliberately geometric because emoji do not render reliably in the bundled font — but the stage glyph already proves the way out: _stage_glyph_sprite is a TextureRect that swaps in bundled art when the stage has it, with the font glyph only as fallback. Knowledge should be a drawn icon on that same seam.

◈ is already taken — it is the Discoveries mark on the faction page. ☰ is the only one that says anything true: three rungs. The two marked RISKY are the coverage gamble the bar's geometric rule exists to avoid, and both need rendering in the bundled font before they are trusted.

custom icon · 28px, then 3×

Cairn

Stacked stones. A trail cairn is inherited practical knowledge — the most on-period reading, and it silhouettes cleanly at 15px.

Rungs

The mechanical truth: every branch is a short ladder and each rung teaches the next. Reads as a ladder — or, unhelpfully, as a menu.

Handprint

The cave-wall mark — ties Knowledge to the Telling's painted voice. The busiest of the three at true size.

All three are drawn here at the button's real 15px and again at 3×, because the whole risk with an icon is that it silhouettes at the size it is judged and mushes at the size it ships. The cairn is my pick — fewest strokes, no clash with ⚒ or the stage hut, and the only one whose meaning is not also the meaning of a hamburger menu. It is the one on the action bar above.

Where, when the answer is a hundred hexes

The overlay picker moves onto the minimap border

A one-turn pulse across a hundred patches is unreadable, and “how long does it stay” has no good answer. Both problems come from making the mark an event. Make it a channel — and put the channel picker where a player will actually find it, which is not inside a modding tool.

nav cluster · press ◐

MINIMAP · the white box is your viewport

Map overlay

Everything in that popover is Inspector → Map → OverlaySection moved intact — the channel list, the per-channel description, the min/avg/max legend and the stub data marker. Ready to climb is one new channel among them. The button is docked on the minimap's top border rather than floated beside it, so it costs the nav cluster no width and cannot be mistaken for a zoom control.

The unlock never lights the map itself. It says what the verb is, and the player who wants to see all hundred and four sources turns the channel on — the same reading at 3 sources or at 300, and nothing anywhere has a duration.

Three things this build decided that the eight-option page could not

It gets wider, not deeper. The rung engine models four rungs; what grows is the number of parallel branches. Six short ladders side by side is a far easier layout than one twelve-deep DAG — and it means the screen never needs pan, zoom, or edge routing.

Provenance is a real field. Three nodes above were not practised: Trail-making was taught by the Ashfolk, Firing was found at a site, and the rest were earned. Once contact can teach you things, “how do I have this” is a question the screen has to answer.

Culture nodes do not unlock verbs. Select Observance or Custom — the detail pane says what changes, not what you may now do. That is the Telling's own “re-colouring, not just unlocking”, and it is why a verb-keyed page could not have held this.
